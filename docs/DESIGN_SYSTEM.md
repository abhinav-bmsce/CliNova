# CLINOVA Healthcare Design System

## 1. Visual Philosophy

The CLINOVA interface is designed to evoke a calm, rigorous, and trusted research environment. Healthcare analytics requires sustained focus; harsh dark modes, over-saturated blues, and noisy dashboards induce cognitive fatigue. CLINOVA employs a **sage-green, warm-white, and muted-earth** palette inspired by botanical therapeutics and sterile research spaces.

## 2. Palette Specification

### Surfaces & Backgrounds
- **Main Background (`#F7F9F6`)**: High-comfort off-white with warm neutral undertones.
- **Secondary Background (`#F0F5EF`)**: Soft sage tint for section wells and chart backdrops.
- **Card & Sidebar Background (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure crisp white for elevated content surfaces.

### Primary & Accent Colors
- **Primary Sage (`#779985`)**: The core brand color, used for primary action buttons, key indicators, and active states.
- **Deep Sage (`#456B56`)**: High-contrast dark sage used for primary headings, prominent icons, and active navigation labels.
- **Soft Sage (`#DFEAE0`)**: Delicate pastel tint used for active item backgrounds, subtle tags, and progress tracks.
- **Muted Teal (`#8BAFA3`)**: Secondary accent for secondary metrics, confidence intervals, and algorithm states.
- **Teal Background (`#E7F1ED`)**: Soft card tint for informational banners and healthcare telemetry tiles.

### Clinical Semantics & Categorical Tints
- **Warm Beige (`#F4EBDD`)**: Used for normative patient clusters, baseline demographics, and neutral badges.
- **Soft Peach (`#F3E5DC`)**: Used for moderate risk indicators, readmission warnings, and elevated thresholds.
- **Lavender (`#EEEAF5`)**: Used for pharmacological categories, comorbidity codes, and exploratory features.

### Typography & Borders
- **Primary Text (`#35463B`)**: Deep woodland charcoal-sage providing WCAG AAA contrast ratio on light backgrounds.
- **Secondary Text (`#758278`)**: Medium slate-sage for supporting labels, timestamps, and column headers.
- **Border (`#E4EAE3`)**: Delicate border line defining card boundaries without visual clutter.

## 3. Component Standards

- **Cards**: Border radius `16px` (`rounded-2xl`), border `1px solid #E4EAE3`, subtle diffuse shadow `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(69, 107, 86, 0.05)`.
- **Navigation**: Sidebar with 260px fixed or collapsible layout, icon + label pairs, soft sage pill indicators for active items.
- **Typography**: Inter / Manrope with weights `400` (body), `500` (labels/buttons), `600` (subheadings), `700` (page titles).
